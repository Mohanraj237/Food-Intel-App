## What the guideline itself recommends

WHO's strong recommendation: adults should reduce sodium intake to **less than 2000 mg/day** (equivalent to less than 5 g/day of salt). This is a **population-level daily-intake guideline**, not a per-product gram threshold — the same reference-intake interpretation step noted in `source-003` applies here too, and must be recorded separately in `rules-v2.md`.
